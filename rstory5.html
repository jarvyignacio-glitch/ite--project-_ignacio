when Stars Stole Our First Kiss

— kenshi iwahashi —

Chapter One: Beneath a Thousand Stars

There are moments in life that seem to exist outside the boundaries of time—moments so beautiful that even years later, we can close our eyes and return to them as if they happened only yesterday. They remain tucked away in the quiet corners of our hearts, untouched by distance, change, or the passing of seasons. For me, that moment happened beneath a sky full of stars. It was an ordinary evening, the kind that carried the gentle warmth of a summer night and the promise of something beautiful waiting just beyond the horizon. The world seemed quieter than usual, wrapped in the soft darkness of midnight, while the stars scattered across the sky shimmered like tiny secrets the universe had chosen to share with only a few. I never imagined that a single night could change the way I understood love. Before that evening, love had always seemed like something distant, something that belonged to the pages of romance novels and the endings of stories I secretly wished were real. I believed in beautiful beginnings, but I was never certain whether such things could happen to someone like me. I had grown accustomed to keeping my feelings hidden, protecting my heart from the uncertainty of wanting someone who might never feel the same way. Then came the person who slowly made those fears feel smaller. There was nothing extraordinary about the way our story began. It unfolded through little moments, quiet glances, familiar smiles, and the kind of comfort that grew so naturally that I barely noticed when friendship began to feel like something more. Somewhere between the laughter we shared and the silences that never felt uncomfortable, my heart learned to recognize a feeling it could no longer pretend was ordinary. It was in the smallest details that I found myself falling. In the way their presence made difficult days feel lighter. In the way a simple moment could become the highlight of an otherwise forgettable day. In the way the world seemed to slow whenever we were together, as if time itself understood that some things deserved to last a little longer. I began to wonder whether they felt it, too. Whether the lingering glances meant something. Whether the quiet moments carried the same unspoken questions. Whether there was a possibility that the feelings I had carefully hidden were no longer mine to carry alone. But love, especially the kind that begins quietly, often lives in the spaces between certainty and fear. And so, I waited. I waited for a sign, for a moment brave enough to change everything, for a reason to believe that the distance between our hearts was smaller than I had imagined. I never knew that the answer would come on a night when the stars seemed brighter than usual. The air was cool, the world was still, and the sky stretched endlessly above us. Beneath that endless blanket of light, the things I had spent so long keeping inside suddenly felt impossible to ignore. There was a strange kind of magic in that moment, as though the universe had quietly arranged everything—the silence, the stars, the closeness, and the feelings neither of us had yet found the courage to name. And then, everything changed. Our first kiss was not something I had planned or imagined in perfect detail. It was a moment suspended between a heartbeat and a breath, a beautiful uncertainty that made the rest of the world disappear. Beneath a thousand watching stars, the distance between us finally vanished, leaving behind a memory that would forever belong to that night. For a moment, I forgot every fear I had ever carried. I forgot the possibility of heartbreak, the uncertainty of tomorrow, and all the reasons I had once believed love was too complicated to trust. There was only the warmth of that moment and the quiet realization that something I had secretly wished for had finally become real. Perhaps that was the most beautiful thing about our first kiss. It wasn't simply the beginning of a romance. It was the moment when all the words we had left unspoken finally found a way to exist between us. But even the stars cannot promise that every beautiful thing will last forever. Somewhere beyond that perfect night waited days we could not predict, truths we had yet to discover, and choices that would eventually test everything we believed about love. The future remained a mystery, hidden behind the same darkness that made the stars shine so brightly. Still, if I could return to that night, knowing everything that would follow, I wonder if I would choose the same moment again. Would I still look up at the stars and wish for something I couldn't explain? Would I still let my heart believe in a love that might one day hurt me? Would I still surrender to a kiss that changed the meaning of every ordinary day that came after it? I think I would. Because some moments are worth remembering, even when they become painful. Some people are worth loving, even when the future remains uncertain. And some kisses become more than memories; they become the beginning of a story that lives inside us long after the night has ended. That night, beneath a thousand stars, I discovered that love doesn't always arrive with promises or certainty. Sometimes, it arrives in a quiet moment, finds its way into an unguarded heart, and leaves us wondering how something so small could change so much. And sometimes, the stars don't simply witness a first kiss. Sometimes, they steal it from the world and keep it among their secrets, preserving the moment when two hearts finally dared to become one. That was the night our story began. And I had no idea how much it would cost us to keep it.
