Chapter One: The Girl Who Saw the Forgotten

The Ethereal Plains were a place caught between life and death. For centuries, the endless silver fields had served as a passageway for wandering souls, guiding the spirits of the departed toward their final resting place. Beneath skies painted in shades of violet and gold, ghostly rivers flowed through fields of glowing flowers, and ancient spirits watched over the boundary between worlds. But when the Moon Gate, the sacred passage that allowed souls to move beyond the mortal realm, mysteriously closed, the balance between life and death began to unravel. Lost spirits wandered without memories, shadows crept into sleeping villages, and strange voices called out to the living from beyond the mist. Luna had always been different from the other villagers. Raised by her father in a quiet settlement near the edge of the plains, she spent her days gathering wild herbs, tending to their little farm, and visiting the abandoned cemetery beyond the hills. Since childhood, she had been able to see the spirits of those who had passed away. Some appeared as faint silhouettes drifting through the streets, while others stood beneath trees, waiting for someone who would never return. Her father taught her to ignore them, warning that the dead belonged to a world she could never understand. Yet Luna could never turn away from a soul that seemed lost or afraid.
