Chapter One: The Melody of Forgotten Stars

The kingdom of Melodia was losing its music. Once, every song carried a touch of magic, from the lullabies that healed broken hearts to the melodies that made flowers bloom beneath the winter snow. For centuries, the kingdom had been protected by the Songkeepers, gifted individuals who could weave spells through their voices and command the very elements with a single note. But when the legendary Song of Creation disappeared, magic began to fade. Birds stopped singing, enchanted rivers fell silent, and an eerie stillness settled over the land. The royal court declared music forbidden, fearing that the ancient melodies might awaken the darkness sleeping beneath the kingdom. Aria had never understood why her voice seemed different from everyone else's. Raised in a small village nestled among rolling green hills, she spent her days helping her father at the village bakery and singing to herself while gathering wildflowers in the meadows. Whenever she sang, strange things happened. Wilted plants lifted their heads, wounded animals grew calm, and gentle breezes danced around her as though following an invisible rhythm. Her grandmother always listened in silence, her eyes filled with a sadness Aria could never understand. She warned her never to sing beyond the walls of their cottage, insisting that some songs were too powerful to be heard by the wrong ears.
