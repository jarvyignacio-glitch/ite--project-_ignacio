Chapter One: The Voice Beyond the Trees

The Faerie Woods held secrets older than any kingdom. For centuries, its enchanted trees had guarded the boundary between the human world and the realm of faeries, where rivers shimmered with moonlight and ancient magic breathed beneath every leaf. But when the silver bells of the forest fell silent, strange things began to happen. Animals vanished without a trace, flowers withered overnight, and a mysterious mist crept through nearby villages, carrying whispers that called people into the darkness. Liora had always been warned to stay away from the woods. Raised in a small cottage at the edge of the forest, she grew up listening to her grandmother's stories about beautiful faerie queens, mischievous forest spirits, and a terrible curse that had trapped the magical realm for generations. Yet Liora had never been afraid of the trees. She often felt as though they recognized her, their branches swaying whenever she passed and their leaves rustling even when there was no wind. Sometimes, just before falling asleep, she heard a soft voice calling her name from somewhere beyond the window.
