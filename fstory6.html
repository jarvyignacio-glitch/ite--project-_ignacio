Chapter One: The Magic Beneath the Roots

The Ancient Grove was dying. For thousands of years, its towering trees had protected the world from the forces of darkness, their roots carrying ancient magic through the earth and their leaves glowing with the power of forgotten spells. Druids once gathered beneath their branches to perform sacred rituals, while mystical creatures roamed freely among the enchanted streams. But when the Heartstone, the source of the grove's magic, mysteriously vanished, the balance of nature began to break. Trees turned to ash, rivers ran dry, and a creeping darkness threatened to consume every living thing. Mira had never believed she possessed magic. Raised in a small village near the edge of the forest, she spent her days collecting wild herbs, helping her aunt prepare remedies, and studying the strange symbols carved into the stones scattered throughout the woods. Unlike the other villagers, she felt no fear when the ancient trees groaned in the wind or when strange lights flickered between their branches. Instead, she felt drawn toward them, as though the forest were calling her home. Sometimes, when she whispered to a dying flower, its petals would bloom again beneath her fingertips.
