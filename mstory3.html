The Silent Lighthouse

— kenshi iwahashi —

Chapter One: The Secret Beneath the Waves

At the edge of a forgotten coastal town stood a lighthouse that had been silent for nearly twenty years. Its white walls had faded beneath the salt of the sea, its windows were clouded with dust, and its iron doors remained locked against the curious and the brave. Every night, waves crashed against the jagged rocks below, carrying the sound of the ocean into the narrow streets of the town. Yet no matter how dark the sky became, the lighthouse never shone its light. The townspeople had long learned to avoid the place. Some believed it was cursed, while others insisted that the lighthouse held the memory of a tragedy no one dared to remember. Years ago, on a night when the sea swallowed the horizon, the lighthouse keeper disappeared without a trace. By morning, his home had been abandoned, the lantern room stood empty, and a small fishing boat had washed ashore with no one aboard. The authorities searched the coastline for weeks, but the missing keeper was never found. After that night, strange things began to happen. Fishermen claimed to see a faint glow behind the lighthouse windows, even when its power had been disconnected for years. Travelers passing along the cliffs sometimes heard a bell ringing through the fog, though no bell had hung in the tower for generations. And on certain stormy nights, the ocean seemed to grow unnaturally quiet, as if the waves themselves were listening for something buried beneath the water. As time passed, the story of the missing keeper became little more than a warning told to children. The older residents preferred to forget, and the younger ones dismissed the lighthouse as an abandoned relic of the past. But the truth had never disappeared. It had only been hidden beneath layers of silence, fear, and years of carefully guarded secrets. Then, one autumn evening, a violent storm struck the coast, tearing through the town and flooding the narrow roads. When the rain finally faded and the tide began to retreat, something unexpected appeared among the rocks beneath the lighthouse: a rusted metal box, dragged from the depths of the sea. Inside lay a collection of water-damaged photographs, a brass key, and a journal whose final pages had been torn away. The photographs showed the lighthouse as it had looked decades earlier, bright and alive beneath the afternoon sun. But in every photograph, there was one unsettling detail—a shadowy figure standing behind the glass of the lantern room. The figure's face was impossible to distinguish, yet its presence appeared in pictures taken years apart, always in the same place, always watching. By midnight, a light flickered inside the abandoned tower. It lasted only a few seconds, but it was enough to awaken a fear the town had buried for twenty years. Curtains closed along the shoreline. Doors were locked. Old memories returned to those who had spent their lives pretending to forget. Far beyond the cliffs, something moved beneath the dark surface of the sea. And for the first time in two decades, the silent lighthouse had begun to speak.
