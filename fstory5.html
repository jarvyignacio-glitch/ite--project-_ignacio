Chapter One: The Voice Beyond the Trees

The realm of Drakoria was disappearing. Once a magnificent kingdom where dragons soared above golden mountains and ancient castles stood among clouds, it had fallen beneath a terrible curse. For centuries, dragons and humans had lived together in harmony, bound by a sacred pact that protected the realm from dark magic. But when the Dragon King vanished and the royal kingdom was swallowed by a mysterious mist, the dragons disappeared one by one. Villages crumbled, ancient magic faded, and the once-great realm became nothing more than a forgotten legend. Kael had never believed in those legends. Raised by a blacksmith in a quiet village near the Ashen Mountains, he spent his days repairing tools, caring for horses, and dreaming of a life beyond the village walls. Yet strange things had always happened around him. Horses grew calm at his touch, flames burned brighter whenever he was angry, and he often dreamed of a magnificent dragon with silver horns calling to him from beyond a sea of clouds. His adoptive father always dismissed the dreams, warning him never to wander toward the mountains, where no traveler had ever returned.
