Whispers of Magic

— kyle macapagal —

Chapter 1: The Silent Woods

The forests beyond Oakhaven had always been quiet—too quiet. Old tales said magic once bloomed there like wildflowers, weaving light through the trees and song into the streams. But that magic had fallen silent a generation ago, locked away by a forgotten vow.

Lira knew the stories well. Her grandmother had taught her to listen—to the rustle of leaves, the murmur of stones, the things most people never noticed. “The magic isn’t gone,” she would say. “It’s only holding its breath.”

On the morning of her seventeenth birthday, Lira found something half-buried beneath the roots of an ancient oak: a crystal pendant, faint and dull, yet humming softly against her skin. The moment her fingers closed around it, the woods sighed. A light glimmered in the distance—pale silver, flickering between trunks—calling her deeper.
