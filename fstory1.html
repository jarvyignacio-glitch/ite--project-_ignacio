Chapter 1: The Empty Cradle

The kingdom of Valoria was falling. For three generations, the great dragons had kept the skies safe and the lands fertile—until the last royal dragon vanished without a trace. Since that day, shadows had crept across the valleys, crops withered, and the throne sat heavy under a cloud of fear.

Elara had never known she was royalty. Raised in a quiet village at the edge of the Whispering Woods, she spent her days tending to goats and listening to the wind sigh through pine boughs. Yet something stirred within her—a restlessness, a voice like distant thunder that spoke only in her dreams.

On the night she turned eighteen, a dragon came. Not the terrible beast of old tales, but a creature wounded and weary, its scales the color of burnished bronze. It landed in her grandmother’s garden, folded its great wings, and fixed eyes like molten gold upon her.
