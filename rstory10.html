Where the Ocean Met Your Eyes

— kenshi iwahashi —

Chapter One: The Place Where Everything Began

There is something about the ocean that makes a person believe in beginnings. Perhaps it is the way the waves return to the shore no matter how far they retreat, or how the horizon stretches endlessly into the distance, promising that there will always be something beyond what we can see. Maybe it is the sound of the water meeting the sand, a gentle reminder that even the things that leave us can find their way back again. For me, the ocean had always been a place of escape. Whenever life became too complicated, I found comfort in its endless blue waters and the quiet rhythm of the waves. It was the one place where I could sit with my thoughts without feeling the need to explain them, where sadness felt a little lighter and uncertainty seemed easier to accept. Beneath the open sky, I could pretend that the world was not asking so much of me. I never imagined that one day, the ocean would become the place where I found someone who would change everything. It happened during a summer I had hoped to forget. I arrived in a small seaside town carrying more than just a suitcase. I brought with me the weight of unfinished goodbyes, the disappointment of dreams that had fallen apart, and the quiet exhaustion of pretending that everything in my life was exactly where it needed to be. I wanted distance from familiar streets, familiar faces, and the memories that seemed to follow me no matter how hard I tried to move forward. All I wanted was a little peace. The town offered exactly that. Its narrow streets were lined with small houses painted in faded shades of blue and white, while fishing boats rested quietly along the shore. The mornings smelled of saltwater and freshly brewed coffee, and the afternoons unfolded beneath a sky so wide and bright that it seemed impossible to remain unhappy for long. Life moved slowly there. People greeted one another as they passed, children ran barefoot along the beach, and the golden light of sunset turned the ocean into a sea of shimmering colors. Everything felt softer, as though the town existed outside the ordinary rules of time. For the first time in months, I allowed myself to breathe. I began spending my afternoons walking along the shore, collecting little shells and watching the waves disappear beneath my feet. I found comfort in the simple things: the cool breeze against my skin, the warmth of the sand, and the quiet beauty of a world that continued to exist even when my own seemed uncertain. Then, one afternoon, I saw you. You stood near the water's edge, your figure outlined by the fading sunlight. The breeze moved gently through your hair, and the waves curled around your feet before slipping back into the sea. There was something about the way you looked at the ocean, as though you were searching for an answer hidden somewhere beyond the horizon. But it was your eyes that I remembered most. They held a kind of quiet depth I could not explain, something that reminded me of the sea itself—beautiful, mysterious, and carrying more beneath the surface than anyone could easily understand. There was warmth in them, but also a sadness that felt strangely familiar, as though you, too, had come to the ocean hoping to leave something behind. I did not know your name. I did not know your story, the dreams you carried, or the memories that had brought you to that same stretch of shore. I knew nothing about the person standing beneath the evening sky, and yet, for reasons I could not understand, I found myself wanting to know everything. Perhaps it was curiosity. Perhaps it was simply the magic of a beautiful evening. Or perhaps there are moments when something inside us recognizes a person long before we understand why they matter. After that day, the ocean no longer felt like a place where I went to be alone. It became a place filled with possibilities, where the ordinary rhythm of the waves seemed to carry the promise of something new. Every sunset offered another chance to return, and every visit left me wondering whether our paths would cross again. Slowly, the thought of seeing you became part of my days. I began noticing the little things I had once overlooked: the changing colors of the sky, the patterns left by the tide, and the way sunlight danced across the water just before evening. The world had not changed, yet somehow, everything seemed more beautiful. It frightened me how easily a stranger could awaken feelings I had spent so long trying to silence. I had come to the coast to escape the past, not to invite someone new into my life. I had promised myself that I would keep my heart safe, that I would not allow another person to become the reason my happiness depended on something beyond my control. But hearts rarely follow the plans we make for them. Sometimes, healing begins when we least expect it. Sometimes, the person who changes our lives arrives when we are still learning how to put ourselves back together. And sometimes, the place we visit to escape our loneliness becomes the place where we discover that we were never meant to carry everything alone. I did not know what you would eventually mean to me. I did not know whether our lives would become intertwined or whether you would remain a beautiful memory belonging to a summer that would eventually fade. I could not imagine the conversations we would share, the secrets we might reveal, or the feelings that would slowly grow between us. All I knew was that, whenever I remembered that first afternoon, I could still picture the ocean behind you and the sunlight reflected in your eyes. It was a simple moment, one that anyone else might have forgotten before the day ended. But somehow, it stayed with me. Perhaps because the ocean had always taught me that some things were worth waiting for, even when we could not see what was coming. Perhaps because your eyes reminded me that even the deepest waters could hold a quiet kind of beauty. Or perhaps because, without realizing it, I had begun to hope for something I had once believed I no longer needed. A new beginning. A reason to stay a little longer. A person who might make the world feel like somewhere I belonged. And so, beneath the endless sky, where the waves met the shore and the horizon disappeared into the fading light, my story began to change. I had come to the ocean hoping to forget. Instead, I found a pair of eyes I would spend a long time remembering. And somewhere between the sea and the sky, I began to discover that sometimes, the most unexpected journeys do not take us away from ourselves. They lead us back to the parts of our hearts we thought we had lost.
