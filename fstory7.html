Chapter One: The Girl Born Between Two Skies

The world of Elarion was divided by an ancient mystery. Beyond the eastern mountains lay the Sun Kingdom, a land of golden cities, endless summer, and powerful fire magic. Across the western sea stood the Moon Kingdom, where silver towers rose beneath eternal starlight and shadows held secrets older than time itself. For centuries, the two kingdoms had remained separated by a magical barrier, their rulers bound by a fragile peace. But when the portals connecting their worlds began to awaken, strange storms swept across the land, and an ancient prophecy foretold the return of a power capable of bringing both kingdoms to ruin—or uniting them forever. Lyra had always felt that she belonged somewhere beyond the ordinary world. Raised in a quiet village between the mountains and the sea, she spent her days gathering wildflowers, sketching the constellations, and wondering why the sun and moon sometimes seemed to follow her wherever she went. Her mother had disappeared when she was a child, leaving behind only a silver pendant engraved with a crescent moon and a golden sun. Whenever Lyra held it beneath the light, the symbols shimmered, and a strange warmth spread through her chest. Her grandmother warned her never to remove the pendant, refusing to explain the secrets hidden within it.
