Chapter 1: The Empty Cradle

The kingdom of Asteria was fading. For centuries, the stars had blessed its lands with magic, protecting its people from darkness and keeping the ancient balance between the mortal world and the realm of faeries. But when the Celestial Crown disappeared, the kingdom began to crumble. Rivers lost their silver glow,

enchanted forests fell silent, and shadows crept beyond the borders of the forgotten realm. Elara had never known she was destined to save it.Raised by her grandmother in a quiet village near the Whispering Woods, she spent her days gathering herbs, tending to their little garden, and watching the stars from her bedroom window.

Yet strange things had always followed her. Flowers bloomed wherever she walked, silver lights danced at her fingertips when she was afraid, and every night, she dreamed of a magnificent crown floating beneath a sky full of falling stars.
