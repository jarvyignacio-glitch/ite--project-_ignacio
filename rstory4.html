The Summer We Found Us

— kenshi iwahashi —

Chapter One: A Summer to Remember

Some summers are meant to be forgotten, while others stay with us long after the sun has set and the days have grown cold. They leave behind memories that time cannot erase, people who change the way we see the world, and feelings that remind us what it means to be alive. For me, that summer was different from all the others. It began with an unexpected journey to a quiet coastal town, where the ocean stretched endlessly beneath the golden sunlight and the breeze carried the sweet scent of salt and summer flowers. Everything seemed peaceful there, almost as if time had slowed down just enough to let people breathe, dream, and discover the things they had forgotten along the way. I arrived with a heart full of uncertainties, carrying the weight of things I couldn't explain and memories I wasn't ready to leave behind. I thought the summer would be nothing more than a temporary escape from the life I had grown tired of—a season of quiet afternoons, empty beaches, and days spent counting down until I could return home. I never imagined it would become the beginning of something unforgettable. Somewhere between the endless blue skies, the sound of waves crashing against the shore, and the warmth of sunsets that painted the horizon in shades of gold and pink, I began to see life differently. The days felt lighter. The nights seemed brighter. And the little things I had once overlooked slowly became the moments I wanted to hold on to forever. It was also the summer I met someone who made the ordinary feel extraordinary. Someone who reminded me that happiness didn't always arrive with a grand entrance. Sometimes, it appeared quietly, hidden in familiar places, unexpected laughter, shared silences, and the simple comfort of having someone beside you when the world felt too heavy. I didn't know it then, but some people enter our lives not to give us all the answers, but to help us find the pieces of ourselves we have lost. And perhaps that was what made the summer so special. It wasn't just about falling in love, making memories, or experiencing the kind of happiness that seemed to exist only in movies and stories. It was about learning to let go of the past, embracing the uncertainty of the future, and discovering that sometimes, finding another person also means finding yourself. Of course, not every beautiful moment lasts forever. The sunsets eventually disappeared beyond the horizon. The warm days slowly turned into distant memories. And the promises made beneath the summer sky would one day be tested by time, distance, and the unexpected turns of life. But that summer taught me something I would carry long after it ended: some people become part of our lives in ways we never expect, and some memories remain precious even when the moment itself is gone. Looking back, I realize that I hadn't traveled to that little town simply to escape my life. I had gone there to begin again. To discover who I was beyond my fears, beyond my regrets, and beyond the person I thought I needed to be. And somewhere along the way, beneath the endless summer sky, I found something I hadn't even known I was searching for. I found love. I found courage. And most importantly, I found us. Because sometimes, the most beautiful stories don't begin when we find the person we love. They begin when we finally find ourselves
